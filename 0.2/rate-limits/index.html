Rate limits

For: whoever runs a server that several clients share. You'll find: the limit on calls a second and the limit on work at once, per caller and per role, and what a client sees past them.

So one client can't take a server for itself, it can limit each caller in two ways. Both are off by default.

Setting Default What it limits
RATE_LIMIT_PER_SECOND 0 (no limit) Calls a second each caller may make
RATE_LIMIT_BURST 0 (twice the rate, at least 1) How many calls may come at once before the rate applies
RATE_LIMITS none Another rate for some callers or roles: name=rate[/burst], role:name=rate[/burst]; 0 for no limit
MAX_RUNNING_PER_CALLER 0 (no limit) The heavy calls one caller may have running at the same time: the application says which (queries, loads, ...) 
RATE_LIMIT_PER_SECOND=20                       # every caller: 20 calls a second, in bursts of 40
RATE_LIMITS=ingest=0,role:analyst=5/10         # the ingest key: no limit; analysts: 5 a second, bursts of 10
MAX_RUNNING_PER_CALLER=4                       # and no more than 4 heavy calls at once
  • The rate is a token bucket per caller: it fills at the rate, up to the burst, and each call takes one.
  • Who a caller is: a key's or a token's name (the one in the logs), or a browser session's. With no keys, the caller's address: behind a reverse proxy, the address it passes on (UVICORN_FORWARDED_ALLOW_IPS).
  • Which rate applies: a caller's own in RATE_LIMITS wins over its role's, which wins over RATE_LIMIT_PER_SECOND.
  • A malformed RATE_LIMITS entry stops the server at startup, naming it.

Past a limit

The call answers 429, with a Retry-After in seconds and the limit it hit, and does nothing:

{"error": "too many calls from 'ann': 5 a second at most (RATE_LIMIT_PER_SECOND, RATE_LIMITS)"}
{"error": "'ann' already has 4 queries running (MAX_RUNNING_PER_CALLER=4): wait for one to finish"}

What isn't limited

The application decides: both leave health checks and metrics out, since load balancers, container health checks and Prometheus call them often. The admin login has a limit of its own (Admin login).

Limits

  • Each server counts on its own. A caller sent to several servers gets the rate at each, and a server that takes over from another starts afresh.
  • No quotas over time, such as rows a day: a reverse proxy, or the application in front, can add them.
